Summary

  • The app collects nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking code, and no network code: it never connects to the internet, and nothing you record or do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else by the app.
  • Your recordings and settings stay on your Mac. We never receive them.
  • The only personal information we ever receive is what you choose to send us by email (for example, a support question).
  • We don’t sell or share personal information, and we don’t use it for advertising.

Personal information we collect

Through the app: none

The app works entirely on your Mac. Recordings are saved as movie files in the folder you choose (Movies › Capture Plus unless you pick another). What’s recorded is what you choose: a display or an area of it, system audio (on unless you turn it off), and your microphone if you turn it on (on its own audio track). The app normally keeps its own windows out of recordings, and records only while a recording is running (the menu-bar icon shows the time and the floating controls are on screen). Your settings (frame rate, countdown, audio choices, shortcuts, the folder you chose), the last area you recorded on each display, and a few first-run notes are kept in the app’s own preferences on your Mac (inside ~/Library/Containers). None of this is sent to us. If you save recordings to a folder that syncs (such as an iCloud Desktop & Documents folder), your sync service uploads them like any other file, under its own terms.

The app asks macOS for these permissions, which you can change at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security and System Settings › Notifications:

  • Screen Recording: to record your screen and system audio, only while you record.
  • Microphone (optional): used only while you record with the microphone turned on.
  • Notifications (optional): to tell you a recording was saved, with buttons to show or open it.
